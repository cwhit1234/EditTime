# Changelog

## [1.0.1] - 2026-11-27

### Added
- Bar Width slider for the Cast Bar once it's unlocked from the Player Frame, so it can be made
  wider without changing its height.

### Fixed
- Only Tank and Healer Power Bars no longer leaves an empty strip where the power bar was. The
  health bar now fills that space.

## [1.0.0] - 2026-11-25

### Added
- Match Width and Match Height. Size the cast bar, Personal Resource Display, experience and
  reputation bars, swing timer bars, damage meter, chat frame, bags and micro menu to any other
  Edit Mode frame. The match follows the other frame as it changes size.
- Visibility options (Always, In Combat, Out of Combat, Hidden) for the minimap, objective
  tracker, experience and reputation bars, bags and micro menu.
- Raid frame growth, horizontally and vertically, including growing from the center.
- Hide Own Frame for the raid frames and raid style party frames.
- Hide Party Title for the raid style party frames.
- Hide DPS Role Icons and Only Tank and Healer Power Bars for the party and raid frames.
- Keybind Text on the action bars: Default, Short or Hidden.
- Experience Text and Reputation Text: Current / Max, Percentage, or both.
- Movers for frames Edit Mode doesn't cover: Target of Target, Focus Target, Toasts and Loot
  Rolls, with Edit Mode's grid and frame snapping.
- Growth Direction for the toasts and loot rolls.
- Every setting is saved per Edit Mode layout and follows layout renames, copies and deletes.
